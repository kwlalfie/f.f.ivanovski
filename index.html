About

'f.f.ivanovski' is the stage-name of the artist/musician alfie newman, born in the black country, and raised on the border of rural herefordshire.

f.f.ivanovski's most recent album 'home.' was written mostly whilst travelling through central europe, with the remaining portion being written and recorded within his university halls in manchester.
